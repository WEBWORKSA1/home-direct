    Water Heater Replacement Cost in 2026

    A standard 40–50 gallon tank water heater costs $1,300–$3,000 to replace, including haul-away. Tankless gas units run $2,800–$5,500 because of venting and gas-line upgrades, and heat-pump water heaters land between $2,500 and $5,000.

    What drives the price

    Type and fuel

    Tankless and heat-pump units cost more up front but use less energy.

    Code upgrades

    Expansion tanks, seismic straps, drain pans and venting may be required.

    Location

    Attic or tight closet installs add labor.

    Capacity

    Larger households need bigger tanks or higher-flow tankless units.

    Emergency timing

    Same-day replacement after a leak usually costs more.

    Signs you need water heater replacement

    • Tank older than 10 years
    • Rusty or discolored hot water
    • Rumbling or popping noises
    • Water pooling around the base
    • Hot water running out faster than before

    DIY or hire a pro?

    Skilled DIY

    Like-for-like electric tank swaps are DIY-able for experienced homeowners. Gas units involve gas lines and venting where mistakes risk carbon monoxide — most areas require a permit and many require a licensed plumber.

    How to save on water heater replacement

    • Replace proactively around year 10 to avoid flood damage.
    • Flush the tank yearly to extend life.
    • Check the anode rod every 3–5 years.
    • Ask your utility about heat-pump water heater rebates.

    Questions to ask before you hire

    1. Does the price include permit, haul-away and code upgrades?
    2. Will my gas line and venting support a tankless unit?
    3. What is the first-hour rating of the quoted model?
    4. What warranty covers the tank versus labor?
    5. Can you install today if needed?

    Water Heater Replacement FAQ

    Tank or tankless?

    Tankless saves space and energy and lasts longer, but costs 2–3× more to install. Payback is best for gas households with high usage.

    How long does a water heater last?

    Tanks last 8–12 years; tankless units can reach 20 years with descaling.

    Do I need a permit?

    Most jurisdictions require one, especially for gas units.
