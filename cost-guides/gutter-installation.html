    Gutter Installation Cost in 2026

    Gutters are cheap insurance for your foundation, siding and landscaping. Seamless 5-inch aluminum gutters cost $8–$15 per linear foot installed, so most homes pay $1,400–$3,200. Copper, 6-inch commercial profiles and gutter guards raise the price.

    What drives the price

    Material

    Aluminum is the value standard; copper lasts generations but costs 3–4× as much.

    Size

    6-inch gutters move about 40% more water — worth it on large or steep roofs.

    Height

    Second-story runs need taller ladders and more time.

    Fascia condition

    Rotted fascia must be replaced before new gutters can hang.

    Guards

    Micro-mesh guards cut cleaning but add significantly to cost.

    Signs you need gutter installation

    • Water spilling over during rain
    • Sagging or pulling away from the fascia
    • Peeling paint or rot behind gutters
    • Pooling water or erosion near the foundation
    • Rust spots or leaking seams

    DIY or hire a pro?

    Skilled DIY

    Sectional vinyl gutters are a weekend DIY on a one-story house. Seamless aluminum is formed on-site by a machine, so it is a pro-only product.

    How to save on gutter installation

    • Extend downspouts at least 4–6 feet from the foundation.
    • Bundle gutters with a roof replacement to share setup costs.
    • Ask for hidden hangers every 24 inches (closer in snow country).
    • Skip cheap guards — mesh quality determines whether they work.

    Questions to ask before you hire

    1. What gauge aluminum is quoted (0.027" or heavier)?
    2. How far apart are the hangers?
    3. Is fascia repair included or priced separately?
    4. How many downspouts and where?
    5. What warranty covers leaks at seams and corners?

    Gutter Installation FAQ

    Are seamless gutters better?

    Yes — fewer joints mean fewer leaks. Seams are only at corners and downspouts.

    Do I need gutter guards?

    If you have overhanging trees, guards can pay for themselves in cleaning costs within a few years.

    How often should gutters be cleaned?

    Twice a year, and after storms if trees are nearby.
