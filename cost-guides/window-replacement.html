    Window Replacement Cost in 2026

    Replacing windows cuts drafts, noise and maintenance. Standard vinyl double-hung windows run $450–$1,250 each installed, so a 10-window project typically costs $6,000–$13,000. Wood, fiberglass, custom shapes and full-frame installs cost more. Note: the federal 25C energy-efficiency tax credit ended for windows installed after December 31, 2025, so check state and utility rebates instead.

    What drives the price

    Insert vs. full-frame

    Inserts sit in the existing frame; full-frame removes everything to the studs and lets the installer fix rot and flashing.

    Glass package

    Triple-pane and low-E coatings add 10–20% but matter most in cold climates.

    Size and shape

    Arches, oversized and custom units are made to order and cost far more.

    Access

    Upper-floor windows need ladders or staging.

    Lead paint

    Homes built before 1978 require EPA RRP lead-safe practices, adding cost.

    Signs you need window replacement

    • Fog between panes (failed seal)
    • Drafts or frost on the inside of the glass
    • Sashes that won't stay open or lock
    • Soft or rotting sills
    • Noticeable street noise

    DIY or hire a pro?

    Skilled DIY

    Insert (pocket) replacements are manageable for a careful DIYer; full-frame installs with new flashing and exterior trim are better left to pros so the water barrier stays intact.

    How to save on window replacement

    • Replace the worst windows first — the payback on a few failed units beats a whole-house job.
    • Compare U-factor and SHGC ratings, not brand names.
    • Ask installers about off-season pricing (late winter).
    • Check your utility for window rebates now that the federal credit is gone.

    Questions to ask before you hire

    1. Is this an insert or full-frame installation?
    2. What U-factor and SHGC does the quoted glass have?
    3. How do you flash and seal the opening?
    4. Is interior and exterior trim included?
    5. What do the product and installation warranties cover?

    Window Replacement FAQ

    Do new windows pay for themselves in energy savings?

    Rarely on savings alone — expect 10–15% lower heating and cooling bills when replacing single-pane units. Comfort, noise and resale are the bigger wins.

    How long does installation take?

    A crew usually installs 8–12 insert windows per day.

    Is the window tax credit still available?

    The federal 25C credit ended for property placed in service after December 31, 2025. Some states and utilities still offer rebates.
