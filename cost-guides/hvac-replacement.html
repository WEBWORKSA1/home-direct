    HVAC Replacement Cost in 2026

    Replacing a furnace and central air conditioner together typically costs $8,000–$16,000; a cold-climate heat pump system runs $9,000–$20,000. Price depends on tonnage, efficiency (SEER2/AFUE), ductwork condition and installation complexity. The federal 25C credit for heat pumps ended after December 31, 2025, so look for utility rebates.

    What drives the price

    Sizing

    A Manual J load calculation prevents oversized equipment that short-cycles and wastes money.

    Efficiency

    Higher SEER2/HSPF2 units cost more but cut operating costs.

    Ductwork

    Leaky or undersized ducts can erase the efficiency of new equipment.

    Fuel switch

    Converting from oil or adding a heat pump may require electrical upgrades.

    Season

    Emergency replacements in heat waves or cold snaps cost more.

    Signs you need hvac replacement

    • System older than 15 years
    • Repairs costing more than 30% of replacement
    • Uneven temperatures room to room
    • Rising energy bills
    • R-22 refrigerant in an older AC

    DIY or hire a pro?

    Pro only

    Refrigerant handling requires EPA certification, and gas, electrical and venting work requires permits. Hire a licensed HVAC contractor.

    How to save on hvac replacement

    • Insist on a written Manual J load calculation.
    • Replace in spring or fall when contractors offer off-season pricing.
    • Check utility rebates for heat pumps and smart thermostats.
    • Compare total installed price and warranty length, not just equipment brand.

    Questions to ask before you hire

    1. Did you perform a load calculation, and what size did it recommend?
    2. What SEER2/AFUE/HSPF2 ratings are quoted?
    3. Is duct sealing or modification included?
    4. Who registers the manufacturer warranty?
    5. What labor warranty do you provide?

    HVAC Replacement FAQ

    Repair or replace?

    Use the 5,000 rule: multiply repair cost by system age. If it exceeds $5,000, replacement usually makes more sense.

    Are heat pumps good in cold climates?

    Modern cold-climate heat pumps work efficiently down to −5°F to −15°F.

    How long does installation take?

    A like-for-like swap takes one day; new ductwork can take three or more.
