    Kitchen Remodel Cost in 2026

    A midrange kitchen remodel typically costs $25,000–$60,000; a cosmetic refresh (paint, counters, hardware) can be done for $15,000, and high-end gut renovations exceed $85,000. Cabinets usually eat 30–35% of the budget, and moving walls or plumbing is what turns a remodel into a major project.

    What drives the price

    Layout changes

    Moving sinks, ranges or walls adds plumbing, gas, electrical and permits.

    Cabinet grade

    Stock, semi-custom and custom cabinets differ by 2–4×.

    Counters

    Laminate, quartz and natural stone vary widely.

    Appliances

    Pro-style ranges and panel-ready fridges add thousands.

    Structural surprises

    Old wiring, rot and asbestos appear once walls open.

    Signs you need kitchen remodel

    • Not enough storage or counter space
    • Cabinets with failing hinges or water damage
    • Outdated wiring or too few outlets
    • Inefficient layout for how you cook
    • Planning to sell in a competitive market

    DIY or hire a pro?

    Skilled DIY

    Demo, painting, backsplash tile and cabinet hardware are DIY-friendly. Moving plumbing, gas or electrical, and countertop fabrication need licensed trades.

    How to save on kitchen remodel

    • Keep plumbing in place to save $5,000–$15,000.
    • Reface cabinets instead of replacing if boxes are solid.
    • Hold a 10–15% contingency for surprises.
    • Order cabinets and appliances before demolition to avoid delays.

    Questions to ask before you hire

    1. Is this a fixed-price or cost-plus contract?
    2. Who manages the schedule and subcontractors?
    3. Which allowances (tile, fixtures, counters) are included?
    4. How are change orders priced?
    5. Who pulls permits and schedules inspections?

    Kitchen Remodel FAQ

    How long does a kitchen remodel take?

    Plan on 6–12 weeks of construction, plus design and ordering time.

    What recoups the most at resale?

    Minor, midrange updates typically recover more (around 70–90%) than luxury overhauls (around 40–55%).

    Can I live at home during the remodel?

    Yes — set up a temporary kitchen with a microwave, kettle and fridge.
