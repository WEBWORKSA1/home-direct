    Deck Building Cost in 2026

    A new deck adds usable living space for far less than an addition. Pressure-treated wood decks run about $28–$45 per square foot built, while composite boards push that to $40–$60+. A 320-sq-ft deck typically costs $9,000–$19,000 depending on height, railings and stairs.

    What drives the price

    Decking material

    Composite costs more up front but needs no staining.

    Height

    Second-story decks need larger posts, beams and bracing.

    Railings

    Cable or glass rail systems can cost as much as the decking.

    Footings

    Frost depth and soil determine footing size and labor.

    Extras

    Built-in benches, lighting and pergolas add quickly.

    Signs you need deck building

    • Soft or rotting boards and joists
    • Wobbly railings or posts
    • Rusted or missing ledger flashing and fasteners
    • Deck pulling away from the house
    • Splinters and cupped boards that won't hold stain

    DIY or hire a pro?

    Skilled DIY

    Ground-level decks are a classic DIY. Raised decks attached to the house need a permit, a correctly flashed ledger and footings below the frost line — mistakes here cause collapses.

    How to save on deck building

    • Keep the footprint rectangular — angles and curves add labor fast.
    • Use hidden fasteners only where they matter; face screws are cheaper.
    • Build in late fall or winter for better contractor availability.
    • Ask for a ledger detail drawing on the permit set.

    Questions to ask before you hire

    1. How is the ledger attached and flashed to the house?
    2. What footing depth and post size are specified?
    3. Is the permit and inspection included?
    4. Which decking and railing products are quoted?
    5. Who handles final inspection sign-off?

    Deck Building FAQ

    Do I need a permit for a deck?

    Almost always for decks attached to the house or more than 30 inches above grade.

    Is composite worth it?

    If you'd otherwise stain every 2–3 years, composite usually breaks even within about 8–10 years.

    How long does a wood deck last?

    15–20 years with regular sealing; composite 25–30+ years.
