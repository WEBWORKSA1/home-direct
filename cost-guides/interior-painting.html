    Interior Painting Cost in 2026

    Professional interior painting runs about $1.80–$4.50 per square foot of floor area, or $400–$1,200 per average room. Painting a whole 1,500-sq-ft home's walls typically costs $3,000–$7,000; adding ceilings, trim and doors increases the total.

    What drives the price

    Prep and repairs

    Patching, sanding and priming add time.

    Ceiling height

    Vaulted ceilings require scaffolding.

    Color changes

    Dark-to-light changes need primer and extra coats.

    Trim detail

    Crown molding and wainscoting are slow to cut in.

    Occupied home

    Moving furniture and protecting floors adds labor.

    Signs you need interior painting

    • Scuffs and stains that won't wash off
    • Peeling or cracking paint
    • Dated or dark colors
    • Patchwork touch-ups that don't match
    • Preparing to list your home

    DIY or hire a pro?

    DIY-friendly

    Interior painting is the classic DIY project: low risk and big payoff. Pros are worth it for high ceilings, stairwells, heavy drywall repair or lead paint in pre-1978 homes.

    How to save on interior painting

    • Choose a scrubbable eggshell or satin finish for living areas.
    • Use the same white for all trim to simplify touch-ups.
    • Paint before new flooring goes in.
    • Neutral colors photograph best for listings.

    Questions to ask before you hire

    1. How many coats are included?
    2. What prep and repairs are included?
    3. Which paint brand and line?
    4. Is furniture moving and floor protection included?
    5. Are ceilings and trim priced separately?

    Interior Painting FAQ

    How much paint do I need per room?

    About one gallon per 350–400 sq ft of wall per coat; use our paint calculator.

    How long does interior paint last?

    Five to ten years, depending on traffic.

    Is it cheaper to paint the whole house at once?

    Yes — setup costs are shared and many painters discount larger jobs.

    Resale value: Neutral fresh paint is the cheapest pre-sale upgrade.
