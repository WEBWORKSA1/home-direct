    Basement Finishing Cost in 2026

    Finishing a basement costs about $25–$70 per square foot, or $20,000–$56,000 for 800 square feet. Adding a bathroom ($10,000–$25,000), wet bar or egress window for a legal bedroom pushes the total higher. Waterproofing must come first.

    What drives the price

    Moisture control

    Waterproofing and vapor management are non-negotiable.

    Ceiling height

    Low ceilings or ducts may need soffits or drop ceilings.

    Bathroom

    Below-grade bathrooms may need an ejector pump.

    Egress

    Bedrooms require code-compliant egress windows.

    HVAC

    Extending ducts or adding mini-splits keeps the space comfortable.

    Signs you need basement finishing

    • Need for a guest suite, office or rec room
    • Dry basement with 7+ ft ceiling height
    • Unused storage space
    • Growing family
    • Rental-unit potential (where zoning allows)

    DIY or hire a pro?

    Skilled DIY

    Framing, insulating and painting can be DIY if you're experienced. Electrical, plumbing (especially a bathroom) and egress windows require permits and often licensed trades.

    How to save on basement finishing

    • Waterproof and run a dehumidifier for a season before framing.
    • Use rigid foam insulation against concrete, not fiberglass with plastic.
    • Plan electrical and data runs before drywall.
    • Check zoning before planning a rentable suite.

    Questions to ask before you hire

    1. How will you handle moisture and insulation against concrete?
    2. Are permits and inspections included?
    3. How will HVAC be extended?
    4. Is an egress window required for my plans?
    5. What allowances are in the bid?

    Basement Finishing FAQ

    Does a finished basement count as square footage?

    Appraisers usually list below-grade space separately, but it still adds value.

    How long does finishing take?

    Four to ten weeks depending on scope.

    Can I add a rental unit?

    Possibly; it depends on zoning, egress, ceiling height and separate-entrance rules.
