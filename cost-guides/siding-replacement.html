    Siding Replacement Cost in 2026

    New siding changes curb appeal more than almost any other project and protects the wall assembly behind it. A typical 1,800-sq-ft exterior costs $10,000–$18,000 in vinyl and $14,000–$25,000 in fiber cement. Removal of old siding, house wrap, trim and the number of corners and openings decide where you land.

    What drives the price

    Material

    Fiber cement and wood cost more but resist fire and impact better; vinyl wins on price and maintenance.

    Tear-off

    Removing old siding exposes sheathing; budget 5–10% for rot repair on older homes.

    Architectural detail

    Gables, bump-outs and many windows add trim and labor time.

    Stories

    Second- and third-story walls require staging or lifts.

    Season

    Vinyl must be installed with expansion gaps calibrated to temperature — reputable crews schedule carefully.

    Signs you need siding replacement

    • Warping, buckling or loose panels
    • Soft or rotting boards when pressed
    • Peeling interior paint or wallpaper on exterior walls
    • Rising heating and cooling bills
    • Fading so severe the color no longer matches repairs

    DIY or hire a pro?

    Skilled DIY

    Vinyl is DIY-able on single-story homes if you can work safely on ladders and handle J-channel details. Fiber cement is heavy, needs silica-dust control and is best left to crews.

    How to save on siding replacement

    • Replace siding and windows together — trim details tie in cleanly and you pay for staging once.
    • Ask for a line-item price for rot repair per sheet of sheathing.
    • Choose mid-tone colors on vinyl; very dark vinyl can warp from reflected sunlight.
    • Get the manufacturer's installation warranty, not only the material warranty.

    Questions to ask before you hire

    1. Is removal and disposal of the old siding included?
    2. Which house wrap and flashing tape will you use around windows?
    3. How are corners, J-channel and trim finished?
    4. What is the warranty on labor versus material?
    5. Do you pull the permit?

    Siding Replacement FAQ

    Is fiber cement worth the extra cost?

    In wildfire, hail or high-humidity regions, often yes — it is non-combustible, rot-resistant and holds paint for 10–15 years.

    Can siding go over existing siding?

    Sometimes, but it hides moisture damage and can void warranties. Most pros recommend a full tear-off.

    How long does new siding last?

    Vinyl typically lasts 20–40 years; fiber cement 30–50 years with periodic repainting.
