    Solar Panel Installation Cost in 2026

    Rooftop solar costs about $2.50–$3.60 per watt installed before incentives, or roughly $17,000–$25,000 for a typical 7 kW system. Important for 2026: the 30% federal Residential Clean Energy Credit (Section 25D) ended for homeowner-owned systems installed after December 31, 2025, so payback now depends on your electricity rates, net-metering rules and any state or utility incentives.

    What drives the price

    Incentives

    With 25D gone, state rebates, SRECs and utility programs make or break the economics.

    Electric rates

    The higher your $/kWh, the faster solar pays back.

    Roof

    Replace an aging roof first — removing and reinstalling panels later costs thousands.

    Equipment

    Microinverters and premium panels raise cost but improve shade performance.

    Financing

    Loans and leases add interest or hand tax benefits to the leasing company.

    Signs you need solar panel installation

    • Electric bills over $150/month
    • Unshaded south-, west- or east-facing roof
    • Roof less than 10 years old
    • Local net metering at or near retail rates
    • Plans to stay 8+ years

    DIY or hire a pro?

    Pro only

    Grid-tied systems require electrical permits, utility interconnection agreements and rooftop work. Hire a licensed installer.

    How to save on solar panel installation

    • Get three quotes and compare cost per watt, not monthly payment.
    • Avoid door-to-door pitches promising 'free solar' — read the lease terms.
    • Ask whether the system size matches 12 months of your actual usage.
    • Check if a lease/PPA (whose owner may still claim business credits) beats buying in your state.

    Questions to ask before you hire

    1. What is the total cost per watt after all fees?
    2. Which panels, inverters and racking are included?
    3. What production (kWh/year) do you guarantee?
    4. Who handles permits, inspection and utility interconnection?
    5. What happens to the warranty if your company closes?

    Solar Panel Installation FAQ

    Is the 30% solar tax credit still available?

    Not for homeowner-owned systems: Section 25D ended for installations completed after December 31, 2025. Leased and PPA systems may still benefit indirectly because their commercial owners can claim business credits.

    How long is the payback?

    Commonly 8–14 years in 2026 without the federal credit, shorter in high-rate states.

    Do panels work in snow and cold?

    Yes — panels are more efficient in cold temperatures; snow slides off tilted panels.

    Resale value: Owned systems can add value in high-rate markets; leases can complicate a sale.
