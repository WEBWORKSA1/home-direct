    Garage Door Replacement Cost in 2026

    A new garage door costs $1,000–$2,500 installed for a single steel door and $1,600–$4,500 for a double. Insulated, windowed, wood and custom carriage-house styles cost more. Garage doors consistently rank among the best resale-value upgrades.

    What drives the price

    Size

    Double doors cost more but less than two singles.

    Insulation

    Insulated doors are quieter and help attached garages.

    Style and windows

    Decorative hardware and glass add cost.

    Wind rating

    Coastal areas may require wind-rated doors.

    Opener

    Smart, belt-drive and wall-mount openers cost more.

    Signs you need garage door replacement

    • Dents, rust or warping
    • Noisy, jerky operation
    • Broken springs or cables
    • Drafts in an attached garage
    • Dated look hurting curb appeal

    DIY or hire a pro?

    Pro only

    Torsion springs store enough energy to cause serious injury. Opener swaps are DIY-friendly; full door and spring installation should be done by a pro.

    How to save on garage door replacement

    • Match style to the home's architecture for maximum curb appeal.
    • Choose insulated (R-value 9+) for attached garages.
    • Replace opener at the same time if it's over 15 years old.
    • Ask about haul-away of the old door.

    Questions to ask before you hire

    1. What gauge steel and R-value is quoted?
    2. Are new tracks, springs and weather seals included?
    3. Is haul-away included?
    4. What warranties cover springs, hardware and panels?
    5. Is the door wind-rated if required?

    Garage Door Replacement FAQ

    How long does installation take?

    Usually 4–8 hours for one door.

    Does a garage door add value?

    Yes — it's one of the highest-recovery exterior projects, often recouping most or all of its cost.

    Can I keep my opener?

    Often, if it's compatible and in good condition.
