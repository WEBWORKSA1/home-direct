    How this calculator works

    Estimate = (size × unit cost + fixed costs) × quality × location × complexity.

    Quality multipliers: economy 0.82, standard 1.0, premium 1.38. Location: lower-cost 0.86, average 1.0, high-cost metro 1.22, Canada 1.30 (in CAD).

    Fixed costs cover permits, disposal, setup and similar one-time items; a minimum job charge applies to small projects.

    Pro tips

    • Get three written quotes and compare line items, not totals.
    • Off-season scheduling can save 5%–15%.
    • Hold a 10%–15% contingency for surprises once walls or roofs are opened.

    FAQ

    How accurate is this estimator?

    It gives a planning range. Your home's condition, access, materials and local labor rates decide the final price.

    Why is Canada higher?

    The Canada setting reflects higher material and labor costs plus the currency difference, so results are in CAD.

    Does it include permits?

    Typical permit and disposal costs are built into the fixed-cost component.
