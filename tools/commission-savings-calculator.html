    How this calculator works

    Each scenario subtracts its listing fee (2.75%, 1.5%, $499 flat or about $150 for FSBO marketing) and, if selected, the buyer-agent fee.

    The cash-offer scenario applies a discount to the price but no commissions or repairs.

    Closing costs and taxes apply to all scenarios equally, so they're excluded from the comparison.

    Pro tips

    • FSBO saves the most only if you price correctly and sell for close to market.
    • Discount brokers often deliver most of the service for half the fee.
    • Treat a cash offer as a floor and negotiate.

    FAQ

    What is a flat-fee MLS listing?

    A licensed broker lists your home on the MLS for a fixed fee (typically $300–$600) while you handle showings and negotiation.

    Do FSBO homes sell for less?

    Some studies show lower average prices, often because FSBO homes are smaller or priced without data. Good pricing narrows the gap.

    Are commissions negotiable?

    Always. In the U.S. there is no standard rate.
