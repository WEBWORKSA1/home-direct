    How this calculator works

    Resale value added = project cost × recovery percentage. Recovery values are typical national midpoints drawn from published remodeling cost-versus-value surveys and appraisal practice; local markets vary widely.

    ROI = (value added + energy savings − cost) ÷ cost.

    Projects buyers see first — curb appeal and entry points — tend to recover the most.

    Pro tips

    • Before listing, prioritize paint, flooring refinishing, garage and entry doors.
    • Don't over-improve beyond the neighborhood's price ceiling.
    • Keep receipts — improvements raise your cost basis for capital gains.

    FAQ

    Which renovations add the most value?

    Garage door and entry-door replacements, minor kitchen updates, siding and refinishing hardwood consistently recover the highest share of cost.

    Do pools add value?

    Rarely their full cost; in hot climates they can help, elsewhere they may narrow your buyer pool.

    Should I renovate before selling?

    Fix defects and refresh cosmetics; skip big remodels unless comparable homes require them.
