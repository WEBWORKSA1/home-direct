    How this calculator works

    Both payments use standard amortization. Break-even = closing costs ÷ monthly savings.

    Lifetime interest saved compares remaining interest on the current loan with total interest on the new loan, minus upfront costs.

    Resetting to a new 30-year term can lower the payment while increasing total interest — compare a 20- or 15-year term too.

    Pro tips

    • Ask for a no-closing-cost option and compare it against paying points.
    • If you plan to move within 3 years, a refinance rarely pays.
    • Keep your remaining term similar to avoid stretching debt.

    FAQ

    How much should rates drop before refinancing?

    Many borrowers look for 0.75–1 point, but the break-even math matters more than a rule of thumb.

    Does refinancing hurt my credit?

    A hard inquiry causes a small, temporary dip. Rate-shopping within a short window counts as one inquiry for most scoring models.

    What is a cash-out refinance?

    Replacing your mortgage with a larger one and taking the difference in cash — useful for big renovations, but it raises your balance.
