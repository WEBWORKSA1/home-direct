    Transparency

    Home.Direct earns revenue from advertising, sponsorships and fees some service providers pay to receive homeowner requests. Donations are optional and do not affect cost data, rankings or which providers we match. Major sponsors are listed here with permission.

    Is my donation tax-deductible?

    No. Home.Direct is an independent business, not a registered charity, so contributions are not tax-deductible.

    How is the money used?

    Support goes to the four funds you choose: operations (hosting, data, tools), promotions and marketing, hiring writers/reviewers/creators, and contest prizes. Sponsors are acknowledged on this page with permission.

    Can I donate prizes or products instead?

    Yes — use the sponsor form to offer gift cards, tools or services for the monthly contest.

    Is payment secure?

    Donations are processed by PayPal. We never see or store your card details.
