    Maintenance FAQ

    How often should I replace my HVAC filter?

    Every 1–3 months for 1-inch filters; every 6–12 months for 4–5-inch media filters. More often with pets or allergies.

    What maintenance prevents the most expensive damage?

    Water management: clean gutters, working sump pumps, sealed windows, and catching small leaks early prevent most big-ticket repairs.

    Does my checklist save?

    Yes — checkmarks are stored in your browser on this device for the current year. Nothing is uploaded.
