    Frequently asked questions

    Quick answers about matching, pricing data, privacy and how Home.Direct works.

    Is Home.Direct free?

    Yes, for homeowners. We're supported by ads, sponsorships, provider fees and optional donations.

    How many companies will contact me after I request quotes?

    Up to three providers who serve your area.

    Do you sell my information?

    We share the details you submit only with the providers matched to your request, and as described in our Privacy Policy. We don't sell lists.

    Are your cost guides accurate for my city?

    They're national typical ranges with regional multipliers. Your local market, home condition and materials decide the final price — always compare written quotes.

    Do you work in Canada?

    Yes. Forms accept Canadian postal codes, calculators include a Canada mortgage mode and estimators can show CAD.

    How often is content updated?

    Rates, news and videos refresh automatically several times a day. Cost guides are reviewed on a rolling basis.

    Can I advertise or sponsor?

    Yes — see Advertise or For Pros, or use the partnership link at the top of every page.

    How do I delete my information?

    Send a privacy request through the contact form and we'll process it as required by applicable law.
