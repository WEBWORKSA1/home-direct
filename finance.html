    Mortgage FAQ

    Are these the rates I'll get?

    No — they're national weekly averages from Freddie Mac's Primary Mortgage Market Survey. Your rate depends on credit, down payment, loan type, points and lender.

    Is Home.Direct a lender?

    No. We're an information and matching service. If you request offers, we share your request with licensed lenders or brokers who serve your area.

    When does refinancing make sense?

    When you can recoup closing costs through lower payments before you expect to sell or refinance again — use the break-even calculator. A rate drop of 0.75–1 point is a common rule of thumb.

    HELOC or cash-out refinance?

    A HELOC keeps your existing first mortgage and charges a variable rate on what you draw; a cash-out refi replaces the whole mortgage. If your current rate is far below today's, a HELOC usually costs less.

    How do Canadian mortgages differ?

    Canadian fixed rates compound semi-annually, terms are typically 5 years within a 25–30-year amortization, and insured mortgages require CMHC or private default insurance under 20% down. Our mortgage calculator has a Canada mode.

    Home.Direct is not a lender, mortgage broker or financial advisor. Rate data are national averages provided for information only. Loan offers come from third parties and are subject to their underwriting and licensing.
