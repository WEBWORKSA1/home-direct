    FAQ

    Questions homeowners ask us

    Is Home.Direct really free for homeowners?

    Yes. Guides, calculators and matching are free. We earn from advertising, sponsorships and fees some providers pay when they receive a request — never from you.

    How does pro matching work?

    You describe the project once. We share it with up to three local providers who serve your ZIP or postal code; they contact you with quotes. You decide whether to hire anyone.

    Can I sell my house without an agent here?

    Yes. Our Sell Direct hub compares cash offers, for-sale-by-owner, flat-fee MLS and discount brokers, and can connect you with cash buyers or low-fee options in your area.

    Where do your cost numbers come from?

    Typical installed-cost ranges compiled from published contractor cost guides, supplier pricing and industry surveys, adjusted with regional multipliers in our estimators. Local quotes vary.

    Do you serve Canada?

    Yes. Forms accept U.S. ZIP codes and Canadian postal codes, and our calculators include Canadian mortgage compounding and CAD estimates.

    How current is the information?

    Mortgage rates, housing news and videos refresh automatically several times a day; cost guides are reviewed on a rolling basis.

    Ready when you are.

    One short form. Up to three local quotes. You stay in control.
