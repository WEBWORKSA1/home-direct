    About Home.Direct

    Home decisions involve big numbers and too many middlemen. We give homeowners the numbers first — then a direct line to the people who can help.

    What we do

    Home.Direct publishes free cost guides for 24 common home projects, twelve calculators for buying, borrowing, selling and renovating, a month-by-month maintenance planner, and curated housing news and videos that refresh automatically. When you're ready, one short form connects you with up to three local providers — contractors, cash buyers, low-fee listing options or licensed lenders.

    How we make money

    Using Home.Direct is free. We earn revenue from display advertising (Google AdSense), clearly labeled sponsorships, and fees some providers pay when they receive homeowner requests. Optional donations support operations, promotion, hiring talent and contest prizes. None of these change our cost data or calculator math.

    How we build cost data

    Guides list typical U.S. installed-cost ranges for 2026, compiled from published contractor cost guides, supplier pricing and industry surveys, with notes for Canadian pricing. Estimators apply transparent multipliers for size, quality, location and complexity. Mortgage rates come from Freddie Mac's weekly Primary Mortgage Market Survey via the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis (FRED).

    What we are not

    We are not a contractor, real estate brokerage, lender, mortgage broker, insurer or financial advisor. Always verify licenses, get written quotes and consult qualified professionals for your situation.

    Get in touch

    Use our contact form. For sponsorship, advertising, partnership or domain inquiries, contact our partnerships team.
