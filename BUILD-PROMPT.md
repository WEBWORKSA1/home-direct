# Home.Direct — Phase-wise Build Prompt

This is the master prompt used to build **Home.Direct**. Run the phases in order; each phase lists exactly what to produce and how to verify it. It also works as a template for any other exact-match "home" domain: swap the inputs in Phase 0.

---

## Concept: the most profitable use of Home.Direct

**Positioning:** *"Go direct. Every home decision, minus the middleman."* It's a single hub for the three largest money decisions homeowners make, all monetized through **lead generation first, AdSense second**:

| Pillar | What the visitor gets | What it earns |
|---|---|---|
| **Hire direct** | 24 cost guides with estimators → multi-step "Get up to 3 quotes" form | Home-service leads. Shared leads sell for roughly $25–$75 each, and the average home-services paid-search lead costs about $91 (roofing about $228), so contractors already pay these prices. |
| **Sell direct** | Cash offer vs. FSBO vs. flat-fee MLS vs. agent comparison, a sell-method quiz and the net-proceeds math → seller form | Seller leads, the highest-value lead type, for cash buyers, iBuyers and discount brokers |
| **Finance direct** | Live weekly Freddie Mac rates, mortgage/refi/HELOC/affordability calculators → lender form | The highest-CPC AdSense inventory on the site (mortgage, refinance, HELOC) plus lender referrals |

**Why this beats a content-only site.** Illustrative assumptions at 50,000 sessions a month:

- **AdSense:** about 110k pageviews × $10–$25 RPM gives roughly $1,100–$2,750 a month.
- **Leads:** about 2% form completion gives 1,000 leads. If 40% are sellable at $40 average, that's roughly $16,000 a month.

Leads are the engine and AdSense is the floor. Sponsorships (exclusive ZIP territories, category sponsors) and donations sit on top.

**Brand rule:** always written **Home.Direct**, with the dot. Never "HomeDirect" (see the trademark disclosure in Phase 3).

## Phase 0: Inputs and hard constraints

- Domain: **Home.Direct**. Repo `webworksa1/home-direct`, served from the **main** branch root on GitHub Pages (free plan, static only).
- AdSense publisher ID `ca-pub-6620975821265271`. Put this exact snippet in the `<head>` of every content page:
  `<script async src="https://pagead2.googlesyndication.com/pagead/js/adsbygoogle.js?client=ca-pub-6620975821265271" crossorigin="anonymous"></script>`
  Also add the `google-adsense-account` meta tag and an `ads.txt` file at the root.
- Top of every page: "Contact, if you are interested in this website / domain name / Sponsorship / Advertisement / Partnership", linked to the partnership contact URL (`PARTNER_URL` in `scripts/hd_core.py`). That URL must never be shown as visible text; it lives only inside the link.
- One inbox for every form, pledge and donation. It must **never** be visible on the site or in page markup: store it encoded in `assets/js/config.js` and decode it only at submit/click time.
- Avoid "homedirect" trademarks and copyrighted material; publish a trademark/copyright disclosure.
- Free to host and run: no servers, no paid APIs, no secrets.

## Phase 2: Design system

- **Palette:** ink navy `#0d1b2a`, signal coral `#ff5a36` (primary CTA), direct teal `#0e7c74`, gold accent, warm paper `#fbf8f3`.
- **Type:** Fraunces for display, Inter for UI.
- **Look:** 14–30 px radii, soft shadows, automatic and manual dark mode, reduced-motion support.
- **Components:** pill buttons, option-tile radios, segmented controls, range sliders, calculator result panels (dark), comparison tables, stat tiles, steps, FAQ accordions, a lite YouTube facade, a sticky mobile CTA, a consent bar and a search overlay.

## Phase 3: Templates, compliance and legal

- **Every page:** semantic HTML, canonical URL, Open Graph/Twitter tags, JSON-LD (Organization, WebSite, BreadcrumbList, FAQPage, Article, WebApplication), favicon/manifest, AdSense snippet. Skip AdSense on the thank-you and 404 pages (no publisher content).
- **AdSense policy:** original, substantial content on every indexable page; no ads inside forms or modals; no incentivized clicks; Privacy Policy includes the Google cookie and partner-sites disclosures with opt-out links; ads labeled.
- **Consent bar:** offers personalized or non-personalized ads (sets `requestNonPersonalizedAds`). Enable Google's certified CMP (AdSense › Privacy & messaging) for EEA/UK visitors.
- **Disclosures page:** not affiliated with HomeDirect, Inc. (HOMEDIRECT USA), the HOMEDIRECT MORTGAGE mark owner, Homes Direct, Inc. or any similar name. Also covers the copyright notice, third-party content (YouTube, publishers, FRED), advertising/compensation disclosure and the not-professional-advice notice.

## Phase 4: Lead-generation engine (the money)

- **Multi-step forms** (`.hd-form` + `.step` fieldsets) with a progress bar, per-step validation, auto-advance tiles and prefill from URL params. Quick hero/sidebar forms pass service and ZIP and skip to step 2.
- **Quote form** (4 steps): service + ZIP → scope, property type, ownership → timeline, budget, details, photo link → contact + consent.
- **Seller form** (4 steps): address/ZIP/type → beds, baths, sq ft, condition → timeline, reason, preferred method, price, mortgage → contact.
- **Lender form** (3 steps): goal + ZIP → value, loan, credit, use, timeline → contact.
- **Lead scoring** (timeline, budget, ownership, phone, motivation) produces **HOT / WARM / COOL**, written into the email subject: `[Home.Direct] HOT · Pro quote request · roofing · 30301`.
- **Attribution captured:** landing page, referrer, UTM/gclid/fbclid, page, timestamp, timezone.
- **Spam defense:** honeypot `_honey` plus a 2.5-second time trap.
- **Delivery:** FormSubmit AJAX endpoint (`/ajax/<inbox>`), with a hidden-iframe POST fallback and an inline success panel with next steps. US ZIP and Canadian postal codes both validate.
- **Consent language:** TCPA-style; consent is not a condition of purchase.

## Phase 5: Cost guides (24)

Each guide carries these data fields:

- low / typical / high
- unit costs, fixed costs and minimum job (feed the estimator)
- size slider
- a cost-breakdown table
- 5 price factors
- signs you need it
- DIY verdict
- an embedded verified YouTube video
- money-saving tips
- questions to ask
- 3 FAQs (schema)
- resale note
- related guides
- an inline quote CTA and a sticky sidebar quote form

Estimator formula: (size × unit + fixed) × quality (0.82 / 1 / 1.38) × location (0.86 / 1 / 1.22 / Canada 1.30 in CAD) × complexity (0.9 / 1 / 1.2).

Tax-credit accuracy: the 25C and 25D homeowner credits ended for property placed in service after December 31, 2025.

## Phase 8: Community and secondary monetization

- **Donations:** four funds (Operations · Promotions & Marketing · Hiring Talent · Contests & Prizes), amount pills or a custom amount. A PayPal Donate URL is built at click time (or uses a hosted button ID). There's also a sponsor/pledge form for large, recurring or in-kind support.
- **Monthly contest:** the theme rotates automatically by month, with a countdown, prize tiers, judging criteria and an entry form (photo links, rules and age consent). The skill-based Official Rules regenerate each month with correct dates. Eligibility: US + Canada, excluding Quebec until registered with the RACJ.
- **Careers / talent network:** writers, licensed contractor reviewers, video creators, partnership reps, moderators, SEO.
- **For Pros:** shared or exclusive ZIP lead signup.
- **Advertise:** Featured Pro, Category sponsor, Newsletter, Contest prize sponsor (indicative rates), plus a partnership and domain-inquiry link.

## Phase 9: Automation (self-updating)

`.github/workflows/auto-refresh.yml` runs every 6 hours, on demand and on script changes:

1. `scripts/refresh.py` updates `data/rates.json` (FRED CSV of Freddie Mac PMMS), `data/news.json` (Google News RSS: title, link and source only) and `data/videos.json` (YouTube channel RSS). Every source fails soft.
2. `scripts/build.py` regenerates every page, the sitemap (lastmod), the search index and the month-dependent content (checklist, contest theme, rules dates).
3. Commits to `main`, and GitHub Pages republishes.

## Phase 10: SEO and performance

Use these:

- `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt`, `ads.txt` and `site.webmanifest`
- descriptive titles and meta tags, breadcrumbs and FAQ schema
- internal linking (related guides and tools)
- the lite YouTube facade (no iframe until play), deferred JS, one CSS file and a `.nojekyll` file

Use relative links everywhere so the site works both on `github.io/home-direct/` and at the `home.direct` root. `404.html` pins its base dynamically.

## Phase 12: Go-live

1. **Enable GitHub Pages:** Settings › Pages › Source: Deploy from a branch › `main` / root.
2. **Custom domain:** Settings › Pages › Custom domain `home.direct` (creates `CNAME`). At the registrar, add apex A records 185.199.108.153, .109.153, .110.153, .111.153 and `www` CNAME → `webworksa1.github.io`. Then tick **Enforce HTTPS**.
3. **Activate form delivery:** submit the Contact form once and click **Activate** in the FormSubmit email. Optionally paste FormSubmit's alias into `config.js` → `formAlias`.
4. **AdSense:** add the site, confirm `ads.txt`, enable Auto ads and the Privacy & messaging GDPR message, then request review.
5. **Search Console:** submit `https://home.direct/sitemap.xml`.
